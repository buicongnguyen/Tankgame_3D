# Easy hull and roadside mines

- Easy player health doubles from its previous setting: 720 → **1,440 HP** before armor upgrades. The multiplier changes from 3× to **6×** Normal and applies to current/max HP on each deployment, retry and training mission. Level-20 armor gives 9,240 HP. Normal, Hard and Crazy retain their existing health; Easy enemies still have half Normal HP.
- Full-size maps keep six mines. Mines no longer occupy the route. Up to one/two/three are placed by alternating shoulders for levels 1/2/3, with remaining mines farther off-route when local cover, objectives or pickups leave insufficient room.
- Clearance includes the full 2.7 m activation circle, the player's 1.25 m collision radius, and a 1 m margin beyond the corridor edge. Placement checks every route leg, so a mine beside one zigzag or loop branch cannot encroach on another.
- Mine positions remain deterministic, reserved against scenery, separated from other supplies, and away from spawn and relay positions. Training and the compact first Easy map remain mine-free. Trigger radius, blast damage, enemy activation and mine visuals are unchanged.

Verification covers all 192 stage/level/difficulty combinations, actual generated cover on all 48 campaign levels, driving along the route beside a mine versus touching its circle, upgraded hull/retries, and desktop/mobile HUD values. Update the balance reference and menu text with the same health multiplier.
